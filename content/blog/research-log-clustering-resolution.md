## The Spark

It all started with a casual conversation. My partner, Luz, mentioned she was preparing to send out her resume for internships. At the time, I was browsing GWM brand cars online and jokingly told her it would be great if she bought one. 

She replied, "Maybe in 2028 we could buy a used one."

That stopped me in my tracks. I blurted out, *"How? I'm not going to be working yet."* 

To which she asked the question that triggered everything: *"What? Then what are you going to do?"*

## The Reality Check

Like many of my classmates, my default assumption was to jump straight into a master's degree after undergrad. But Luz's question hit me with a sudden wave of anxiety. I realized I want to start earning money as soon as I graduate. Learning is a lifelong process that never stops, but I want financial independence because I want to build a stable family. 

That realization led to a cascade of internal questions:
* *Do I have a decent profile?*
* *Am I actually competent in my field?*

## The Solution

I realized I didn't actually know the answer. To figure it out, I needed to organize my thoughts and recap everything I've built. 

That's why I created this webpage. It serves as a central hub to document my work, track my progress, and hopefully improve my mental process in how I design future projects. 

## Why "Vulgaris"?

I named the site in honor of my cat, Frijol (Bean). 

The scientific name for the common bean is *Phaseolus vulgaris*. Since "Phaseolus" doesn't exactly roll off the tongue for most people, I went with **Vulgaris**. 

Beyond the connection to Frijol, the word translates to "common." It perfectly represents the commonality of my everyday projects and reminds me of the simple, foundational elements behind all my work.
