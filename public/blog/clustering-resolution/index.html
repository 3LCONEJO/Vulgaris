Research log: picking a clustering resolution I could defend

2026-02-14 / scRNA-seq, notes

Placeholder content. Swap this for a real log entry — short, dated notes work well here.

Spent most of this week on something that sounds trivial and isn’t: choosing a clustering resolution I could actually defend in a lab meeting, rather than the one that “looked right.”

What I tried

  • Swept resolution from 0.2 to 1.6 and tracked cluster count at each step.
  • Used a stability score (bootstrapped re-clustering) instead of just silhouette width.
  • Cross-checked candidate resolutions against known marker genes for cell types I expected to see.

What I’d tell past-me

Eyeballing a UMAP is a fine starting point, but it isn’t a method. Pair it with something quantitative, even something simple, so the choice survives a “why did you pick that number” question.
